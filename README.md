# Walk in the flower fields

A first-person, procedural flower meadow built with Three.js. The meadow, vegetation, wind, sky, and lighting are generated in code; the project does not require local image or audio assets.

## Controls

- **W / S:** move forward / backward
- **A / D:** intentionally inverted lateral movement (A moves right; D moves left). Movement follows the direction the camera is facing, including diagonally.
- **Shift:** move faster
- **Mouse:** click the canvas to capture the mouse, then move to look around. Press Escape to release it. Dragging also works when pointer lock is unavailable.
- **Touch devices:** use the left joystick to move and drag the right side of the screen to look around.
